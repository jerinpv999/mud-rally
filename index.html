MUD RALLY

Slide a rally car around a muddy forest stage. Race the computer, or friends over a direct peer-to-peer link.

Direct WebRTC connection, no game server. You swap two codes with each friend over any chat app. The host can invite up to 3 players and fill the grid with computer cars.
